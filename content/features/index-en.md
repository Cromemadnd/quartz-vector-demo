This independent demonstration uses an English interface. Article language is selected through the links below; switching articles does not change the interface language. [中文导览](features/)

## Complete sample articles

- [Starsea Library — English](features/infobox-en)
- [星海图书馆 — 简体中文](features/infobox)

Both versions describe a fictional library. Compare the infobox, section contents, category links, and navigation boxes at the bottom of each article.

## Reading and discovery

Use the main menu and contents controls to move panels into the sidebars. Use Appearance to change text size, page width, and color theme. On narrower screens, the interface adapts to a compact layout.

Type “Library” in the search field to try title suggestions and full-text results. Where supported by the interface, hover over article links to inspect page previews. The library article also links to related pages through its graph and navigation boxes.
